# Kexory

**Building technology people can trust.**

Kexory is a technology company focused on developing products and solutions across software, hardware, and artificial intelligence.

We believe technology should be useful, reliable, and thoughtfully designed. Security is an integral part of that vision, built into the architecture and development process from the beginning.

## What We Focus On

- **Software:** Applications, platforms, tools, and digital infrastructure.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Intelligent systems and AI-powered solutions.
- **Hardware:** Computing technologies and hardware-related innovation.
- **Infrastructure:** Cloud services, servers, networking, and systems.
- **Security:** Security by design, privacy, and trustworthy technology.

## Our Approach

We focus on building technology with a long-term perspective.

Our approach emphasizes:

- Security and privacy by design.
- Practical innovation and engineering quality.
- Simplicity, reliability, and transparency.
- Open collaboration and responsible development.

## Open Source

We believe open-source software plays an important role in advancing technology and enabling collaboration.

Through our public repositories, we aim to share useful projects, tools, experiments, and resources with the developer community.

Not all Kexory projects are open source. Each project has its own development model and licensing terms.
